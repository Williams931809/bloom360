About Bloom360

Bloom360 Initiative is a community for girls and women to build confidence, develop practical skills and find guidance for their education, careers and entrepreneurial goals. Through mentorship, learning and conversations, Bloom360 helps participants identify a next step and take it.

Girls exploring education and career paths, recent graduates deciding what comes next, working women developing their careers and aspiring entrepreneurs are all welcome here.

Our mission

Equip girls and women with mentorship, practical learning and access to opportunities so they can make informed decisions about their futures.

Our vision

A future where girls and women have the confidence, support and access to opportunities they need to pursue the lives and careers they choose.
