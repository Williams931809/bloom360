About Bloom360

A community for girls and women building skills, confidence and direction.

Our story

Bloom360 Initiative brings together mentorship, learning and community conversations to help girls and women identify and take their next step.

Mission and vision

Proposed mission. Equip girls and women with mentorship, practical learning and access to opportunities so they can make informed decisions about their futures.

Proposed vision. A future where girls and women have the confidence, support and access to opportunities they need to pursue the lives and careers they choose.

Dr. Thelma Ezeonu

Bloom360 Initiative is led by Dr. Thelma Ezeonu, a physician, entrepreneur and philanthropist.
